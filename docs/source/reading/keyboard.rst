.. _reading-keyboard:

Keyboard shortcuts
==================

An RSM page can be read with the keyboard only. The shortcuts act on the
selected block, which is tinted: click a block to select it, or move to it
with the keys below.

.. list-table::
   :header-rows: 1
   :widths: 25 75

   * - Key
     - What it does
   * - ``j``, ``k``
     - Move to the next or the previous block, link or button.
   * - ``l``, ``h``
     - Move to the next or the previous block at the same level. On a section
       heading, move to the next or the previous section.
   * - ``H``
     - Go to the top of the page.
   * - ``z``
     - Scroll so that the selected block is in the middle of the window.
   * - ``,``
     - Fold or open the selected block.
   * - ``;``
     - On a proof or a step: fold or open all of its steps.
   * - ``.``
     - Open or close the menu of the selected block. In the menu, the up and
       down arrow keys move between the items, and ``Enter`` chooses one.
   * - ``i``
     - On a reference: show or hide its tooltip.
   * - left and right arrow keys
     - On a wide equation: scroll it sideways.
   * - ``Escape``
     - Close the menu, the sidebar panel or the source of a block, and turn
       off the marks of **Depends on** and **Used by**.

``Enter`` or ``Space`` on a folded block opens it. ``Tab`` moves through the
links and buttons of the page in order, as on any web page.

The shortcuts do nothing while you type in a field, such as a field of the
Notation view, or while you hold ``Ctrl``, ``Alt`` or ``Cmd``.

The scroll bar map
==================

An RSM page replaces the browser's scroll bar with a map of the page: a thin
strip at the right edge of the window, with a mark for each section and
figure, and a box over the part of the page on screen.

- Drag the box, or click the strip, to scroll, as with a scroll bar.
- Hover over a mark to see the name of its section or figure, and click it to
  go there.

On a phone, the map shows only while the page scrolls or the map is touched.

The reader can turn the map off in the "Reading" view of the sidebar. An
author can turn it off for the whole document with ``:no-minimap:`` in
``:config:``. Without JavaScript, the page keeps the browser's scroll bar.

The example below scrolls inside its frame, so its map is on the right edge
of the frame.
